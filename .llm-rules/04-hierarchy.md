# 04 - Hierarchy and Emphasis

## General Principles

- **Visual Hierarchy:** Establish clear visual hierarchy. Emphasize important elements.
- **Size:** Don't rely solely on font size. Use weight and color.
- **De-emphasize:** De-emphasize competing elements to emphasize others.

## Labels & Semantics

- **Labels:** Avoid unnecessary labels. Combine labels and values when possible.
- **Document Hierarchy:** Separate visual hierarchy from HTML heading tags.
- **Weight & Contrast:** Balance weight and contrast.

## Actions

- **Semantics (Actions):** Style actions based on importance, not just semantics.
    - Primary: Solid, high-contrast.
    - Secondary: Outline or lower-contrast.
    - Tertiary: Like links.
    - Destructive: Secondary/tertiary on main page, primary in confirmation.
