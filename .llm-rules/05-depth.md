# 05 - Depth and Realism

## Light Source & Shadows

- **Light Source:** Emulate a light source for raised/inset elements.
- **Shadows:** Use shadows to convey elevation.
    - Small, tight shadows: Close to background.
    - Large, blurred shadows: Further away.
- **Two-Part Shadows:** Consider two shadows: a larger, softer one and a tighter, darker one.

## Flat Design

- **Flat Design Depth:** Use color and overlapping for depth even in flat designs.
    - Lighter: Closer.
    - Darker: Further away.
