# 06 - Images

## Quality & Contrast

- **Good Photos:** Use high-quality images.
- **Contrast:** Ensure contrast between text and background images.
    - Use overlays, lower contrast, colorize, or add text shadows.

## Sizing & User Content

- **Intended Size:** Avoid scaling images significantly.
- **User-Uploaded:** Control shape and size of user-uploaded images. Crop and prevent bleed.
